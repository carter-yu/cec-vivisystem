# Philosophy

We treat cec-vivisystem as a **vivisystem** — a blend of the born and the made.

## Guiding Principles (from Kevin Kelly)

- **Grow from the Bottom Up**: Start with the smallest reliable working piece. Only expand what has proven useful in real family life.
- **Maximize Decentralization**: Prefer many simple, focused components over one central intelligent controller.
- **Honor Your Errors**: Failures are expected. Design so that errors are visible, recoverable, and informative.
- **Hive Mind**: Specialized components interact through clear events and boundaries. Intelligence emerges from the interactions.
- **Distributed Control**: We carefully design the environment, contracts, and guardrails. Within those limits, components act freely.

## Practical Consequences
- No long-running central “brain” or hidden orchestrator.
- Shared Google Calendar is part of the external environment.
- Human confirmation is required before any calendar write.
- Development happens in small weekend increments only.
